# Two-dimensional model comparison

The fixed inputs contain the October 2026 analytical partitions for Chile, France and Brazil. Chile preserves its previously studied choice partition. France and Brazil assign whole ranking or approval contexts before deriving strict pairs, remove person–pair overlaps between partitions, remove contradictory comparisons and collapse consistent duplicates. These benchmark partitions are distinct from those used for the main country maps.

Each unordered proposal pair is a response column. A value of 1 selects the lower-ID proposal, 0 the higher-ID proposal, and −1 is unobserved. Preparation uses training responses only. It iterates the same common-support rule for all estimators: at least 20 retained choices per participant, both outcomes per pair and minority share at least 0.025. Full retained supports are 27,163 participants and 4,005 pairs in Chile, 1,188 and 6,931 in France, and 535 and 2,180 in Brazil.

All primary models have two dimensions. B2 is the positive symmetric DYNAMAP head with 105 choice parameters; B1 is its constant-scale spatial ablation; B4 is the historical flexible head with 20 choice parameters; B3 is biased pairwise matrix factorization. W-NOMINATE and Bayesian IRT estimate parameters per comparison rather than sharing a position for each original proposal. Equal data and dimension do not imply equal parameter counts or tuning budgets.

B1–B4 select six configurations by mean validation log loss across seeds 101 and 201. The grid crosses two optimizer/learning-rate settings with L2 penalties 0, 0.0001 and 0.01. B1, B2 and B4 use Adamax at 0.01 or Adam at 0.003; B3 uses Adam at 0.003 or 0.01. Minibatches have 512 choices. Training starts with 80 epochs and patience 10; the supplied code implements the predefined extension to at most 160 epochs. Tuning uses Chile's 5,000-participant condition, France's 1,000-participant condition and Brazil's full condition. Each selected configuration applies to all sizes in that country. Final fits use seeds 301, 401 and 501.

Native W-NOMINATE 1.5 uses two dimensions, minvotes 20, lop 0.025, trials 3 and default utility parameters. Polarity follows training-only anchors. Each condition produces one native point fit. The code saves the native object before postprocessing and verifies prediction parity with the package. It does not certify a global optimum.

Bayesian probit IRT uses pscl 1.5.9 and four chains with seeds 101, 201, 301 and 401. Three training-selected anchors identify the map. Person prior precision is 1; item precision is 0.04. Chains start at 10,000 iterations, use burn-in 5,000 and thinning 10, and extend in blocks of 5,000 to at most 30,000. Rank-normalized split/folded Rhat must be at most 1.01 and bulk/tail ESS at least 400 for the probability probes, nonanchor coordinate probes and all retained item parameters. At the tuning size, separate sensitivities use item precision 0.16 or alternative training-selected anchors. Fits failing the joint diagnostic gate do not receive predictive scores. All original primary and sensitivity conditions reached the iteration cap without satisfying that joint gate.

All final neural configurations and fits for a country are fixed before test prediction. Predictive evaluation uses identical held-out rows. Accuracy gives half credit to ties; log loss and Brier score accompany accuracy. Choice-weighted metrics pool responses, whereas participant-weighted metrics first average within participant. Neural point estimates average fitted-model metrics rather than ensemble predictions. Paired intervals use 1,000 participant bootstrap samples and condition on the fitted models. The external left/right classifier uses fixed folds, scaling within training folds, logistic regression C=1 and the same eligible participants. Political labels never enter preference-model fitting or selection.

The included source retains these statistical settings. The portable release reruns preparation locally and replaces the original server scheduler with explicit stages. Runtime depends on the new hardware; the published runtime summaries remain the measurements from the original workflows. The input archives provide the fixed analysis-ready partitions, not a second raw-data ingestion pipeline. Their file hashes and the source-code lineage are recorded in `inputs.json` and `source_provenance.json`.
