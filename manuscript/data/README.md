This review branch contains the supplied source inputs except for the two pending CSV files listed above, together with the intermediate data generated by the notebooks. Source files are read-only inputs. The intermediates support inspection of the downstream analyses. The Chilean observations came from Chilecracia. According to the authors, the data were originally public and the website later expired. The copy here comes from the study archive supplied by the authors. France and Brazil were collected through MonProgramme2022 and Brazucracia and made available in [Harvard Dataverse](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/8E0EA4). ### Age and ideological coding

Chile retains known ages 19–120. France and Brazil record age categories: code 1 is the indivisible 10–19 group, codes 2–7 represent 20–29 through 70+, and codes 98/99 are unspecified responses. Category 1 is excluded in full. Unknown age is retained in every country. Invalid or conflicting reported ages lead to participant-level exclusion, removing all that person's comparisons. Notebook 01 shows the decisions explicitly. Age-specific plots use only their documented known-age subsets.

Chilean self-placement runs from 0, left, to 10, right. The binary validation uses 0–4 versus 6–10 and excludes 5. France and Brazil use 1–2 versus 4–5 and exclude 3. Ideological labels and demographics are attached after coordinate estimation. In the validation outputs, `target = 0` means left and `target = 1` means right.

## Generated models and analytical outputs

`../outputs/models/` contains sixteen supplied fits: eleven positive symmetric fits and five flexible-head fits. `Chile_dim2_seed101` is the main Chilean map. Other Chilean directories vary dimension or initialization. `france/` and `brazil/` hold the independently fitted country maps. `flexible/Chile_dim2_seed*` holds the five flexible-head fits loaded or regenerated by notebook 04b.

- `model.weights.h5` stores neural weights. `model_specification.json` stores the constructor arguments needed to reload them.
- `coordsU.csv` stores one participant per row, keyed by `uuid` in Chile and `user_id` elsewhere. `coordsP.csv` stores one proposal per row, keyed by `id` in Chile and `option_id` elsewhere. Columns `z1`, `z2`, and any further `z` columns are learned coordinates.
- The main Chilean fit also includes coordinate snapshots at epochs 0, 5, 10 and 20. They describe optimization, not changes in respondents' opinions over time.
- Histories, metrics and model properties describe the saved fits. They remain associated with those fits when `REFIT = False`.

`../outputs/validation/` is generated by 05. `eligible_participants.csv` contains the complete-case classification sample for each country. Fold assignments and `*_oof_predictions.csv` link each participant to a held-out prediction, target and probability of identifying with the right. The four specifications use coordinates, demographics, both, or PC1. Fold metrics, feature importances, ROC points and participation-group summaries are derived from those predictions.

The other output directories hold figures, component panels and tables displayed by their generating notebooks. Notebook 14 writes correlations across initializations. Notebook 17 checks saved-model probabilities and distances. These results are distinct from the raw responses and are overwritten when regenerated.

## Supplied historical comparisons: `benchmarks/`

These are numerical results from the comparison experiments retained in the manuscript. They are inputs to 16, not fresh estimates from the current positive symmetric choice function. The [benchmark README](benchmarks/README.md) maps each file to its table and states which fitting code is outside this package.

Notebook 04b writes flexible-head prediction metrics, and 14b writes its distance and PC1 correlation matrices in `../outputs/ablation/flexible/`. Notebook 18 combines these with the positive symmetric results from 02/04 and 14 to produce the `choice_head_ablation` tables. All of these inputs are included for independent notebook execution.
